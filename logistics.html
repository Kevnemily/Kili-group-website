Moving Tanzania's mining industry safely and reliably

OLTRECK Transporters is the dedicated logistics division of Kilimanjaro Group, providing comprehensive transportation services for the mining industry across Tanzania. We specialize in the safe and compliant movement of hazardous materials, heavy equipment, and industrial goods.
