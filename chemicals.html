Kilimanjaro Chemicals — Mining Chemicals Division

Kilimanjaro Chemicals is the specialized mining chemicals division of Kilimanjaro Group, based in Kahama, Tanzania.

We supply flotation reagents (collectors, promoters, regulators), leaching agents (sodium cyanide, caustic soda, hydrogen peroxide), frothers, depressants, activators, and activated carbon (coconut shell, coal-based, granular) to mining operations across Tanzania.

Applications: gold processing, copper flotation, base metal beneficiation, and effluent treatment.
