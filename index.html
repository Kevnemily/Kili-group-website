About Kilimanjaro Group

Kilimanjaro Group is a Tanzanian mining solutions provider headquartered in Kahama. We supply mining explosives, blasting consultancy, mining chemicals, PPE, industrial equipment, and nationwide logistics.

Partners: Dyno Nobel, WST. Branches: Kahama (HQ), Geita, Dar es Salaam, Tanga, Dodoma, Nzega, Musoma (MMG).
