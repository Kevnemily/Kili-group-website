Product Description

Our Bulk Emulsions are premium-quality explosives formulated for maximum performance in mining and construction applications. Engineered with advanced emulsion technology, they provide superior energy output, excellent water resistance, and consistent results in all blasting conditions.

Manufactured with strict quality control and safety standards, our bulk emulsions are trusted by mining operations across Tanzania for their reliability and efficiency. The product is available in various strengths and formulations to suit specific site conditions and blasting requirements.

Our technical team provides full support with blast design, on-site delivery, and loading services to ensure optimal results for every operation.

Applications

Our Bulk Emulsions are ideal for a wide range of blasting applications in the mining and construction industries, including:

Open Pit Mining Efficient rock breaking in large-scale surface mining operations.
Underground Mining Precise blasting for tunnel development, stoping, and ore extraction.
Quarry Operations Aggregate production with optimized fragmentation for crushing circuits.
Civil Construction Excavation, trenching, and site preparation for infrastructure projects.
Dam & Tunneling Specialized blasting for dam construction and tunnel excavation.
Wet Conditions Effective in water-filled boreholes due to excellent water resistance.
