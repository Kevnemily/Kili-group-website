About General Supplies

Everything your operation needs, delivered fast

Kilimanjaro General Supplies is the dedicated procurement and distribution division of Kilimanjaro Group. We supply a comprehensive range of essential mining products — from PPE and safety gear to industrial consumables and specialized equipment.

With a nationwide branch network and strong partnerships with global manufacturers, we ensure every mining operation has the equipment and supplies they need, exactly when they need them — with uncompromising quality and safety standards.
