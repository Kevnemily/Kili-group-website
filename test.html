Common Questions. ✦
Everything you need to know before your safari
How early should I book a safari?

The earlier the better, especially during high season (June–October). We also help with short-notice bookings whenever availability exists.

Can I customize a safari package?

Yes. Every safari can be tailored to your preferences — duration, accommodations, activities, and more.

Are there family-friendly safaris?

Absolutely. We have a wide selection of family-friendly safaris with child-friendly accommodations and activities.

What if I don't see a package I like?

You can still send a request via our Tailor form. We'll match you with the perfect safari based on your preferences.

What is the best time to visit Africa for a safari?

It depends on the region. East Africa is spectacular during the Great Migration from July–October. Southern Africa offers excellent wildlife viewing year-round.
