Privacy Policy

How Kilimanjaro Group collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 29 September 2026 Compliant with Tanzania PDPA 2022

On This Page

1. Introduction 2. Information We Collect 3. How We Use Information 4. Legal Basis 5. Sharing & Disclosure 6. Cookies & Tracking 7. Data Security 8. Data Retention 9. Your Rights 10. Children's Privacy 11. International Transfers 12. Changes to This Policy 13. Contact Us

1. Introduction

Kilimanjaro Group Ltd ("Kilimanjaro Group", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information about you when you visit our website at kilimanjarogroupltd.co.tz (the "Site"), contact us, or engage with our products and services.

This policy is issued in compliance with the Tanzania Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (PDPA) and applicable regulations. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the Site.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Information you provide to us

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, company name, and branch when you fill out a contact form, request a quote, or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Business information — site details, mining operations, delivery address, and order-related preferences when requesting products or services.
  • Communications — the content of any messages, inquiries, or feedback you send to us by email, phone, WhatsApp, or other channels.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, time spent, and clickstream data.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small text files used to remember preferences, measure traffic, and improve your experience (see Section 6).

2.3 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data such as health information, biometric data, or political opinions through this Site. If any such information is shared with us during commercial engagement, it is handled strictly according to our internal data protection standards.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries, requests for quotes, and support questions.
  • Provide, deliver, and improve our products and services (explosives, chemicals, equipment, logistics, and consultancy).
  • Send transactional communications such as order confirmations and delivery updates.
  • Send newsletters and marketing updates — only where you have opted in (you may unsubscribe at any time).
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Site, including detecting and preventing fraud, misuse, or security incidents.
  • Comply with our legal, regulatory, tax, and safety obligations in Tanzania and other jurisdictions where we operate.

4. Legal Basis

Under the Tanzania PDPA 2022, we process personal data only where we have a lawful basis:

  • Consent — when you voluntarily submit information (e.g., newsletter signup, contact form).
  • Contract — when processing is necessary to fulfill an order or service agreement.
  • Legal obligation — when required by Tanzanian law, including safety, tax, and regulatory requirements.
  • Legitimate interests — for internal security, fraud prevention, and business improvement, provided your rights are not overridden.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers — trusted partners who help us operate (e.g., hosting, analytics, email delivery, cloud storage). These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Business partners — such as our strategic partners Dyno Nobel and WST, where necessary to fulfill your request for products or technical support.
  • Logistics partners — including OLTRECK Transporters, to deliver orders to your site.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities — when we are legally required to do so, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Successors — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) for the following purposes:

  • Essential — required for the Site to function (e.g., session management, security).
  • Analytics — to understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it.
  • Marketing — to measure the effectiveness of campaigns (only where enabled).

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect functionality of certain parts of the Site.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Role-based access controls on internal systems.
  • Regular security reviews of our hosting infrastructure.
  • Secure storage of data with our cloud providers.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. For example:

  • Contact form submissions are retained for up to 3 years for customer relationship purposes.
  • Commercial and transactional records are retained for 7 years to comply with tax and regulatory requirements.
  • Analytics data is retained for up to 26 months.

When no longer needed, information is securely deleted or anonymized.

9. Your Rights

Under the Tanzania PDPA 2022, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we process your data.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Portability — request your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is based on your consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@kilimanjarogroupltd.co.tz. We will respond within the timeframes required by law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Commission of Tanzania.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child, we will take reasonable steps to delete it promptly.

11. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (such as cloud hosting and analytics providers) may process data outside Tanzania. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, consistent with the PDPA and international best practice.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you directly.

Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
